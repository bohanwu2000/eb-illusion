The plug-in interval is the model-based posterior interval. The propagated interval adds the estimation error in μ̂ and γ̂². The sweep shows 95% interval coverage against the number of calibration studies K, holding the other settings fixed, with the nominal 0.95 line and a marker at the current K. Raising the transportability gap δ biases the estimator and collapses coverage. Raising τ² erodes coverage unless the interval is corrected. The bias shape barely affects results, since only the first two moments enter.
